Contract Clarity Review

A fixed-scope, plain-English map of what your business contract actually commits you to. $295 per contract.

What you get

Send a vendor, client, or lease contract. Get back a report listing every financial obligation in it — each one quoted with the exact clause it came from:

  • Payment terms and schedules
  • Late fees and interest charges
  • Renewal clauses, notice windows, and price-increase terms
  • Liability caps and exposure
  • Termination costs and conditions
  • Audit rights and other ongoing obligations

Plus a one-page plain-English summary and a full clause-by-clause appendix.

How it works

  1. You send the contract (PDF or Word) and a one-page intake: the parties and key dates.
  2. The $295 fee is confirmed before work starts.
  3. Machine pre-screening flags candidate clauses; a human reviews every finding before delivery.
  4. You receive the report within 3 business days (contracts under 25 pages).

The method is open source and published — the classifier behind the pre-screening is public, with honest published accuracy figures and limits. The machine never has the last word.

What this isn't

Consulting and documentation only. This is not legal advice, and we are not a law firm. If your contract needs a lawyer, we'll tell you that.
