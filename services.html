Services

Fixed-scope consulting and documentation engagements. Each engagement defines its deliverables and acceptance criteria up front.

Job-Search OS

A structured way to run a job search as an operation rather than a scramble:

  • Intake and organization of roles, employers, and materials
  • Application tracking with evidence at each step
  • Review checkpoints before anything is submitted
  • A complete record of what was done and decided

Workflow & obligation consulting

For work with many moving parts and real obligations attached:

  • Mapping person → responsibility → project → task → deliverable → evidence
  • Decision discipline: separating research from requirements, findings from obligations
  • Review gates so nothing advances on assumptions

Opportunity research & organization

  • Structured research on roles, employers, funding, or markets
  • Findings organized for traceability and human review
  • No black boxes: sources and methods are documented

What this isn't

Consulting and documentation only. We don't do staffing or placement, payroll, legal advice, or tax advice. If an engagement needs those, we'll tell you plainly and point you to someone qualified.
